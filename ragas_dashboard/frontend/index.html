Contrastive Calibration Reference

How your speech will be evaluated against curated standards:

Content & Relevance (25%)

Premise support, factual grounding, scenario responsiveness, depth.

Structure & Coherence (20%)

Opening hook, signposted transitions, logical arc, memorable synthesis.

Fluency & Pacing (20%)

Target 130-160 WPM, purposeful pause placement, filler word density (< 2%).

Vocal Delivery (20%)

Fundamental frequency (F0) pitch dynamic range, absence of flat monotony, SNR.

Readability index, lexical diversity, avoidance of repetitive looping.
